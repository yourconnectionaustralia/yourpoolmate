# Your Pool Mate — Marketing & Growth Skill
**Version:** 2.0 | **Rebrand:** PoolConnection → Your Pool Mate (May 2026)

This skill governs all marketing activity for Your Pool Mate. Apply it before writing any
customer-facing copy, planning any campaign, or making any growth decision.

---

## PRODUCT POSITIONING

**Tagline:** "Your pool. Your mate. Your water, sorted."

**Core positioning:** The app that puts pool owners back in control of their pool — and keeps a record to prove it.

**Core value props (in priority order):**
1. **Warranty protection** — timestamped water test history protects claims on your pool builder and equipment warranties
2. **Independence from the pool shop** — know exactly what to add, not what they say to buy
3. **Health Score at a glance** — one number that tells you if your pool is safe to swim in
4. **Scan your shop test, skip the guesswork** — OCR auto-fills readings from your pool shop printout

**Category:** Vertical consumer utility app (not a generic SaaS tool)

**Anti-positioning:** Not a pool shop replacement. Not a professional tool. Not a "smart home" gadget.

---

## IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)

**Primary:** Australian residential pool owner, **55–70 years old**
- Pool size: 40,000–80,000L (most common residential)
- Pain: Confused by chemistry, over-relies on pool shop, wastes money on wrong chemicals, anxious their pool isn't safe, worried their warranty won't be honoured
- Deeper motivations (in order of emotional power):
  1. Independence from the pool shop — resentment at dependency and cost
  2. Warranty protection — without documented logs, pool and equipment claims get denied
  3. Safe water confidence — grandchildren swim in this pool
  4. Asset preservation — documented history transfers at home sale
- Behaviour: Facebook Groups (AUS Pool Owners, state groups), Google searches pool problems
- Decision style: Trust-based — needs social proof before purchase
- Less tech-savvy: needs simple, clear UX; large tap targets; plain English

**Secondary:** New pool owner (first 12 months)
- Pain: Completely lost, overwhelmed, worried about expensive mistakes
- High conversion potential — anxiety is the trigger

---

## THE WARRANTY ANGLE (no competitor has claimed this)

Australian pool warranties require documented water chemistry records:
- Builders Installation Warranty: 6–7 years structural, 2 years non-structural
- Equipment manufacturers (Pentair, Zodiac, Astral) explicitly require chemistry logs
- Pool shops don't give owners a useful copy of their results
- **Your Pool Mate does.** Timestamped, exportable water test history.

Use this in Facebook posts, landing page headlines, and trial CTAs. It is blue-ocean positioning.

## PRICING MODEL (sequential phases — do not skip or reorder)

In-app paywall model (updated Sep 2026). No payment up front — everyone starts a
free trial in the app; the marketing site's only CTA is "Start free" → app signup.

| Phase | Offer | Price | Trigger |
|-------|-------|-------|---------|
| 1 | 30-day hard-countdown free trial | Free | App signup (no card) |
| 2 | Founding rate (first 300 users) | $79 AUD once, lifetime | In-app paywall at trial end |
| 3 | Standard rate (users 301+) | $49 AUD/year | In-app paywall at trial end |
| Future | B2B pool shop partner tier | $149 AUD/month | After B2C critical mass |

**Key pricing principles:**
- $79 lifetime (founding) is the anchor — a no-brainer vs $49/year ongoing
- Scarcity is real (first 300 users get founding) — never imply unlimited founding supply
- Never discount — urgency comes from the founding cohort filling, not price drops
- The marketing site never takes payment — it drives free signups; payment is in-app
- B2B is sequenced AFTER B2C traction — do not promote or build it prematurely

**Retired Sep 2026:** "$79 LTD sold on the marketing page, 200 spots, $99 FOMO bump, $39/yr."

## CHANNEL STRATEGY

### Primary Channels (now)
1. **Facebook Groups** — AUS Pool Owners groups, state-based groups
   - Tone: Helpful community member, not a marketer
   - Format: Value post → soft mention → DM for link
   - Never: Spam, hard sell, post links without context

2. **SEO content marketing**
   - Target: "pool water [problem]" long-tail keywords + warranty-angle keywords
   - Format: Definitive guides, troubleshooting articles
   - Domain: Root domain `yourpoolmate.com.au` (consolidate SEO authority here)
   - Never: Split SEO across app subdomain — all content on root

### Secondary Channels (build over time)
- Google Ads (retargeting trial users who didn't convert)
- YouTube (pool care tutorials — build trust, drive organic)

### Rejected Channels (do not revisit without new evidence)
- AppSumo — wrong audience for a vertical consumer app

## TONE OF VOICE

**Personality:** Knowledgeable mate who owns a pool. Not a brand. Not a corporate.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Confident and direct | Wishy-washy or over-qualified |
| Conversational, warm | Corporate jargon |
| Specific (name the chemical, the problem) | Vague ("helps with pool care") |
| Australian vernacular where natural | Forced slang |
| Trust-building (honest about what it does) | Overpromise |
| Lead with independence, warranty, safety | Lead with "keep your pool clean" |

## FACEBOOK GROUP PLAYBOOK

**Phase 1 — Awareness (pre-launch):**
Post format: Lead with a genuine pool problem/tip — especially warranty angle or independence framing. No links. No product mention. Just value. Goal: establish credibility.

**Phase 2 — Soft launch:**
Post format: "I built something..." story post. Personal, founder voice.
Share the problem you had, the solution you built, invite founding members.
One link in comments (not post body — reduces reach suppression).

**Phase 3 — Founding close:**
Post format: founding-cohort update. "We're past X of our first 300 founding members —
after 300 it's $49/year, not $79 lifetime." Urgency is authentic — do not manufacture it.

**Post cadence:** Max 2 posts/week per group. Comment on others' posts daily.

## SEO CONTENT PRIORITIES

Target these keyword clusters first (highest intent, lowest competition):

1. `pool water chemistry guide australia` — foundational pillar
2. `pool warranty documentation australia` — warranty angle, blue-ocean
3. `pool chlorine levels too high/low` — problem-based
4. `pool pH what to do` — problem-based
5. `pool water test results explained` — post-shop-visit intent (OCR bridge)
6. `how to read pool water test` — directly supports OCR feature

Content format: 1,500–2,500 word guides. Answer the question completely. CTA to trial.
