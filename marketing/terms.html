🏊The service

Your Pool Mate is a web application that helps Australian residential pool owners track water chemistry, calculate a Pool Health Score, and keep a timestamped record of water tests. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

👤Your account and trial

  • New accounts include a 30-day free trial with full access. When the trial ends, continued access requires a purchase (founding member lifetime access for the first 300 members, or the annual subscription after that).
  • You're responsible for keeping your login credentials secure, and for the accuracy of the readings you enter.

⭐Founding member lifetime access

  • "Lifetime" means for the operating life of the Your Pool Mate service — one payment, no recurring fees, including future features released to consumer accounts.
  • Founding access is limited to the first 300 members and is for personal, non-commercial use on your own pool(s).

↩️Refunds

30-day no-questions-asked refund on founding member purchases: email hello@yourpoolmate.com.au within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

⚠️Guidance, not a guarantee

Always follow chemical product label directions and safety instructions.

  • Health Scores and dosing recommendations are calculated from the readings you provide. They are general guidance for residential pool maintenance — always follow chemical product label directions and safety instructions.
  • Water test records are timestamped as entered. Whether a warranty provider accepts any record is determined by that provider and your warranty's terms — keep your original pool shop test printouts too.
  • The service is not a substitute for professional advice on water safety, equipment, or structural issues.
