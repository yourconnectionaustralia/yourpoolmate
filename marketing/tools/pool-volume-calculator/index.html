How it works

Measure in metres. Average depth is the shallow end plus the deep end, divided by two — the same rule written on Pool setup in the app.

A rectangle then uses the app's own sum: length × width × that average depth × 1,000 = litres.

A round pool uses the area of a circle. An oval uses the area of an ellipse. A kidney or freeform pool is estimated as 85% of the rectangle it fits inside. The app does not calculate those three shapes; this page uses ordinary geometry for them.

Always follow the product label directions. This page only estimates volume. When you add chemicals, follow the label and re-test before adding more.
