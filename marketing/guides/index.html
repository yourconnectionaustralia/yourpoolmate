Ideal pool water ranges (Australia)

Keep these in range and most pool problems never start. Test weekly in the swimming season and fortnightly in winter.

ReadingIdeal range
Free chlorine1.0–3.0 ppm (about 3 ppm uncovered with stabiliser; 1.5–2.0 ppm covered)
pH7.2–7.6
Total alkalinity80–120 ppm
Stabiliser (cyanuric acid)30–50 ppm
Calcium hardness200–400 ppm (vinyl liners: up to 400)
SaltAs your chlorinator manual says, often 3,000–5,000 ppm
Combined chlorineUnder 0.2 ppm; shock at 0.5 ppm or more
